# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

This project expects respectful, direct, and constructive collaboration.

Participants should:

- focus criticism on ideas and technical outcomes
- keep feedback specific and actionable
- assume good intent unless evidence shows otherwise
- respect differences in experience, background, and perspective
- avoid exposing another person's private information

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, abusive language, and bad-faith disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this standard. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's contact details at [ghassan-alhamoud.com](https://ghassan-alhamoud.com). Do not publish sensitive reports in an issue.
